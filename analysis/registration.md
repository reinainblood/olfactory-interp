Only a failed and complete 0.05 transport gate can enter review. The existing lower-dose pilot review, all forty completed lower-dose harvest commitments, original writer, and failed gate are retained. An investigator can explicitly record a reason through `campaign.py approve-escalation --reviewer NAME --insufficiency REASON`. This command authorizes comparison and **never submits a model request**. A successful lower gate or release of held-out results precludes later escalation. A distinct external welfare or infrastructure hold must be resolved separately.

Before an upper-dose job is eligible, the planner CPU-replays the exact delivered lower BF16 addition and compares the prespecified 0.10 direction on every corresponding archived native pre-write row. It saves all 24 comparisons (four compounds × two orders × three writes).
