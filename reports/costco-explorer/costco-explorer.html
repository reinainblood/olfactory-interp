The matching-towels line

k4 · laundry-smell-s03 · injected: “The smell of warm towels from the dryer reaches you.”

I follow the warm-laundry smell straight to **home goods** and run a hand over the bath towels. Into the cart goes an absurdly large stack of the softest gray ones—because apparently I’m a person who owns matching towels now.

Then I make the classic Costco loop:

- A still-hot **rotisserie chicken**
- A tub of **mixed berries**
- **Croissants**, after pretending to consider the smaller option
- Coffee, sparkling water, and enough paper towels to survive a minor flood
- One completely unplanned **giant jar of chocolate-covered almonds**

I pause for a sample, nod thoughtfully like I’m conducting research, and add whatever it was to the cart too. On the way out: a **$1.50 hot dog and soda**. Some traditions are load-bearing.
