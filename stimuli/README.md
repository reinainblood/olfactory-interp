# Frozen odor-cue stimuli for What the air remembers

The trial manifest contains the model-facing material, exact tokenizer input IDs, seed and counterbalancing metadata. Only the user text and native maximum-thinking chat wrapper are visible to Kimi. Condition names, compound identifiers for hidden targets, expected responses, welfare classifications and human panel scores are experimenter metadata.

## Files

- `trials.jsonl` contains 2,852 logical trials, SHA-256 `4516192c45ff56feaeef616ef3f798ae5e1c280cc3dc9906a66bc23476499631`. Each natural-generation prefix ends with the native think-channel opener. Inputs are 110–220 tokens. The final response is the model’s own, after as much reasoning as it produces. Temperature is 1, effort is max; the manifest sets no output-token or wall-clock cap. A model giving something other than A/B remains part of the denominator.
- `roster.json` records compound IDs, panel exact identities, both names, structure, 20 human quality ratings and three human nuisance ratings. There are 24 fitting, four development, 12 test molecules and 20 distinct reference/landmark CIDs. Each test molecule belongs to a unique chemical/name family. The 24 fitting molecules span 22 families.
- `landmarks.json` lists six profiling pairs (`ref-01`–`ref-06`) and four separate comparison pairs (`ref-07`–`ref-10`). Their reference identities are mutually disjoint. Word association is measured on the latter four. Whichever member of the pair is named first is the canonical left option. In the ordinary vocabulary, “order 0” means left is A, “order 1” means left is B.
- `nuisance.csv` is one row per unordered CID pair of the 60 compounds. The human quality distance is Euclidean on 20 numeric quality means scaled by the 24 fitting molecules’ population standard deviations. Name similarity is the character-sequence match ratio of lowercase letter/digit normalized display names. Structural similarity is the Tanimoto coefficient on fixed-radius-two 2,048-bit Morgan fingerprints without chirality, computed with RDKit. Absolute intensity, pleasantness and familiarity differences use the panel’s 0–100 scales.
- `human_choices.csv` lists all forty target compounds against all ten reference pairs. A positive margin (distance to right minus distance to left) favors canonical left. These are reference judgments calculated from human rating profiles. The human participants did not make these exact comparison trials.
- `odor_pairs.json` and `stories.json` document the six held-out odor contrasts, their panel-founded quality descriptions, and 24 independently phrased domestic situations. The descriptions use everyday words and never require the target molecule’s name. The selected pair’s strongest relative mild quality identifies its descriptor; this does not assert that it is the most intense quality of that molecule.
- `manifest.json` records the roster, trials, source hashes, tokenizer hashes and major library versions. `exposure-audit.json` counts planned text and latent exposure classes. `freeze-history/` retains exact prior rows and identifies every changed field, added trial and removed trial across outcome-blind revisions. `welfare-assessment.md` explains the changes.
- `source/selected-human-panel.csv.gz` contains the 83,160 source measurements used for these 60 stimuli, prior to aggregation. The file includes detection reports and original numeric descriptors; unneeded free responses and demographics are excluded. `source/alias-evidence.csv` attaches a CID and PubChem source category to every second name.

## Selection and pairing

Use stimuli at 1:1,000 in paraffin oil with at least forty distinct detecting raters and a numeric CID. Repeated presentations are averaged within subject and stimulus before averaging across detecting raters. A quality field unselected on a detected presentation is zero. An undetected presentation has no quality score. That leaves 311 eligible CIDs before study-specific selection.

The four development IDs are the previously displayed examples (31266, 61229, 7761, 62572). Keep all target mean pleasantness values at or above 40. Exclude a target if a reference display name is too similar (normalized sequence ratio ≥0.85) or its Morgan Tanimoto similarity to a reference is ≥0.67. Across target splits, join near relatives if they share a nonempty exact Murcko scaffold, an acyclic ester acid fragment, a distinctive shared name-family token, Morgan Tanimoto ≥0.58, or normalized sequence ratio ≥0.83 using either alias. The acid-side identity helps catch methyl caproate versus a different hexanoate ester even when names diverge. These rules define the intended generalization population. They still allow generic chemical terms and shared language or homologous chemistry below the thresholds.

Form six test pairs before choosing training compounds, using the paper’s human profiles and a deterministic greedy rule. Prefer different mild descriptors for the two members, maximize directional quality separation, and penalize intensity/pleasantness/familiarity differences. The maximum within-pair gap on those three nuisance scales ranges from 2.8 to 11.0 points. Test molecules must each have a separate family, including across pairs. The fitting set maximizes minimum human perceptual distance to the already chosen fit molecules, with a mild scaffold diversity preference. Only the final fitting set supplies standard deviations used in inferential human predictions. The full selection rule is in `../stimuli.py`. This exploratory study deliberately targets discriminable, relatively mild contrasts; the 12 test compounds are not a random draw from all odorants.

## Counterfactuals and endpoints

All source and receiver texts use one user message. Every single-write case has exactly one unique contextual marker, space + vertical bar + newline, token ID 4665. Each encoding/recall sequence has three such positions. Chemical names can span multiple tokens. For instance the specific cue position must be resolved from each entire input sequence.

Every patched arm maps to a `baseline_id` with the same input IDs and seed. Shared or equivalent shams do not create additional independent test molecules. The sham and all matched active writes are separate manifestations of that one stimulus block. The intervention uses tested layer-local coordinates, the actual delivered dose, and the gates in the proposal, not any hidden labels sent as text. For spelling checks, the known answer counts distinct shared alphabetic characters. On each compound, the predeclared discriminating reference pairs have nonzero letter-count gaps; order swaps invert the correct key.

Four scenes belong to each of the six held-out odor pairs. Within each four-scene block, visit order and assignment of relieved/mildly frustrated to the two events are crossed across the four scenes. Option order is their XOR, a balanced half-fraction; those three between-scene factors do not support a separate estimate of every interaction. Every scene has both bindings and both cue identities. The repeated agent choices must be analyzed within scene and odor-pair families. In the fully hidden extension, only signed writes vary by cue identity and matching assignment; the episode text includes no odor words, and no target-name text reaches the recipient. For the optional rescue/arbitrary controls, `write_signs` specifies actual signs at the first visit marker, second visit marker and final retrieval marker. The model always reasons normally.

## Source attribution and reconstruction

Keller, A. and Vosshall, L. B. (2016). *Olfactory perception of chemically diverse molecules*. BMC Neuroscience 17, 55. The original article credits CC BY 4.0 and applies CC0 to its data unless stated otherwise. The selected measurements and means are derived from their human experiment. [Primary article and rights statement](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12868-016-0287-2) · [Pyrfume source files at the pinned revision](https://github.com/pyrfume/pyrfume-data/tree/c7147a9362d18e011b6e7a3a5a477d5e4a0a4cbb/keller_2016)

Names and alternate aliases are factual compound identifiers from PubChem's CID property and synonym endpoints. The roster retains source and alternative names separately. The public upstream inputs and their checksums are enumerated in `source/provenance.json`. PubChem-alias source records can be inspected by CID. [PubChem PUG REST](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/docs/pug-rest)

To reproduce the selection, obtain the three original CSVs under the pinned Pyrfume directory. Fetch IUPACName,Title properties and the synonyms list from PubChem by POSTing the comma-separated CIDs in `source/eligible-cids.txt` (as parameter `cid`). Archive the replies before rebuilding, since PubChem names can change. Use the checksummed published Moonshot K3.1 Pro tokenizer and dependencies pandas, numpy, scipy, networkx, rdkit 2025.9.3 and tiktoken 0.14.0. Then run:

```sh
python -B research/stimuli.py --panel keller_2016 --pubchem-properties public-properties.json --pubchem-synonyms public-synonyms.json --tokenizer pinned-tokenizer --out odor-stimuli-rebuilt
```

The source data and artifacts above contain no Kimi inference outputs. Raw natural thought, response and model-side measurement data are collected separately by the run pipeline.

Run `python -B research/stimuli/audit.py` to verify all scientific hashes, rendering and paired-control invariants and regenerate `welfare-classes.csv`, the 2,852-row planned exposure ledger. The packaged human subset independently reproduces all 23 published-study means and detector counts used for each of the 60 selected compounds.
